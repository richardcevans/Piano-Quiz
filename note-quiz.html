Circle of fifths cheat sheet
Clockwise goes up a fifth and adds a sharp. Counter-clockwise goes up a fourth and adds a flat. The inner ring is the relative minor, with the same key signature.

Sharps are added in this order: F C G D A E B (Father Charles Goes Down And Ends Battle). The key is one half step above the last sharp.

Flats are added in the reverse order: B E A D G C F (Battle Ends And Down Goes Charles's Father). The key is the second-to-last flat. F is the one exception, with just B♭.
